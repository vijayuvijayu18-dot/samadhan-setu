Impact Measurement Architecture
7-Stage Protocol: How grassroots ideas systematically evolve into verified ground outcomes
Standardized Protocol
1
Problem Identified

Grassroots citizen, panchayat, or self-help group logs an unaddressed local difficulty.

2
Baseline Recorded

Initial ground conditions, pollutant counts, or spoilage rates captured with geotags.

3
Solution Developed

University student squads engineer hardware or software prototypes under mentor guidance.

4
Pilot

Controlled on-ground deployment in selected villages with industry partner resources.

5
Community Feedback

End-user residents, Mukhiyas, and local workers validate usability and effectiveness.

6
Outcome Measured

Calibrated sensor telemetry or lab test assay post-intervention.

7
Verified Impact

Transparent immutable ledger entry recording confirmed percentage delta.

PASSPORT #01 Water Resources Stage 7: Community Feedback
Abandoned Coal Pit Mine Water Purification & Fluoride Remediation
Project ID: #PROJECT-2026-0001
Problem:

Disused opencast coal pit reservoirs hold millions of gallons of water with toxic fluoride exceeding 2.9 mg/L. Tribal and mining hamlets suffer from acute drinking water scarcity and skeletal fluorosis.

Location:

Baghmara & Nirsa Blocks, Dhanbad District, Jharkhand
GPS: 23.7957° N, 86.2084° E • 4 Panchayats

University:

Birla Institute of Technology (BIT Mesra), Ranchi
Dept. of Chemical & Environmental Engineering

Industry Partner:

Tata Steel CSR & Technology Division
Skid fabrication & field instrumentation support

Solution:

Decentralized solar membrane filtration plant with activated alumina fluoride adsorption cartridge and IoT flow telemetry.

Pilot Status:

Active Field Deployment • 90-Day Continuous Operation (Stage 7)

Evidence:
Water Test Cert #BIT-42 Skid Setup Photo IoT Telemetry Log
Community Feedback:
"For the first time in years, the water from our community standpost does not taste bitter or leave white chalk in cooking pots. Our children have clean drinking water."
— Gram Sabha Resolution & Mukhiya Signature, Baghmara
Verified Outcome:
Fluoride Concentration: 80.0% REDUCTION
Baseline: 4.5 mg/L → Post-Treatment: 0.9 mg/L (BIS Safe Limit ≤ 1.0)
PASSPORT #02 Agriculture Stage 5: Prototype Testing
Solar-Assisted Farm-to-Haat Cold Storage & Vernacular Market Linkage
Project ID: #PROJECT-2026-0002
Problem:

Tribal vegetable farmers in Ranchi belts suffer 35% post-harvest rot for tomatoes, peas and cabbage due to absence of village-level pre-cooling, forcing distress sales.

Location:

Bero & Kanke Blocks, Ranchi District, Jharkhand
GPS: 23.2942° N, 85.0831° E • Vegetable Cluster

University:

Birsa Agricultural University (BAU) & NIFFT Ranchi
Agri-Engineering & Thermal Research Squad

Industry Partner:

Adani Agri-Logistics & State Innovation Council
Cold chain equipment & mentor support

Solution:

Modular phase-change material (PCM) solar micro-cold room with vernacular SMS pre-booking system for weekly haats.

Pilot Status:

Pilot Ready / Lab Testing • Controlled Chamber Run (Stage 5)

Evidence:
Thermal Logger Log Cold Room Assembly Farmer SHG Ledger
Community Feedback:
"Storing tomatoes in the micro-chiller for four days enabled our women's SHG to sell on Sunday haat when prices peaked, instead of distress-selling on harvest morning."
— Bero Mahila Kisan Samiti, Ranchi
Verified Outcome:
Post-Harvest Spoilage Rate: 82.8% REDUCTION
Baseline Spoilage: 35.0% → Chamber Spoilage: 6.0% (4-Day Storage Test)
